# LoudEase 0.9.1 Beta 1

Adds a welcome page and three small fixes on top of 0.9.0's automatic mode.

## What changed

- First-run welcome page in 11 languages; a fresh install follows the browser language.
- Transition protection no longer stays engaged on stable high-crest programmes (renews only on a real source-level jump).
- Douyin feed swipes reset the programme estimate in automatic mode too.
- Same-programme dynamics amount 0.86 to 0.92; bounds and safety limits unchanged.

## Evidence boundary

- Full static/DSP suite, automatic-mode e2e 12/12, tab-capture matrix 8/8, stripped-package verification.
- The 0.92 dynamics change was measured on synthetic and real-page numeric runs but not by controlled listening. This is a public beta, not a hearing-protection claim.
