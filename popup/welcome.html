LoudEase is ready

Nothing to set up. Play a video or music and LoudEase evens out loud ads and quiet dialogue on its own.

  1. Open any video or music page and press play.
  2. Look for ON on the toolbar icon. That means LoudEase is balancing this tab.
  3. Pin LoudEase from the puzzle-piece menu to keep the icon in view.

Audio is processed on your device only. If a site's audio cannot be attached automatically, open LoudEase once on that tab to use full-tab capture.
